<h2>VIM (1984) — International Vocabulary of Basic and General Terms in Metrology</h2>
<p>The <strong>International Vocabulary of Basic and General Terms in Metrology (VIM)</strong> was first published in <strong>1984</strong> as a joint publication by BIPM, IEC, ISO, and OIML, issued by ISO as <strong>ISO Guide 99:1984</strong> (also known as <strong>ISO/TAG 4:1984</strong>) and by OIML as <strong>OIML V 2:1984</strong>.</p>
<p>This is the <strong>first edition</strong>, superseded by <a href="/vocab/vim-1984-cor-1987/concept/1.01" target="_blank">OIML V 2:1984/Cor.1:1987</a> and ultimately by <a href="/vocab/vim-1993/concept/1.1" target="_blank">OIML V 2:1993</a>. It provides 138 concept definitions across 6 chapters. The 1984 edition uses a different chapter structure from later editions, with separate chapters for "Measurement results" and "Measuring instruments" instead of the consolidated "Devices for measurement" chapter introduced in 2007.</p>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> A corrigendum was issued in 1987 correcting 3 concepts (1.01, 4.10, 5.23). This dataset preserves the original 1984 text; see <a href="/vocab/vim-1984-cor-1987/concept/1.01" target="_blank">V 2:1984/Cor.1:1987</a> for the corrected text.</p>
