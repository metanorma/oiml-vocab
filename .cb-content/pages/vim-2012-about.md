<h2>About this VIM Concept Browser</h2>
<p>This is an abridged version of the _International vocabulary of metrology - Basic and general concepts and associated terms (VIM)_ (JCGM 200:2012, 3rd edition) (or VIM3), supplemented by annotations for some of the entries. It is an online, interactively browsable document produced by Working Group 2 of the Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM/WG 2).</p>
<p>With respect to the <a href="http://www.bipm.org/en/publications/guides/vim.html" target="_blank">VIM3 version that can be freely downloaded in pdf format</a> from the <a href="http://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm" target="_blank">BIPM site, JCGM section</a>, this annotated version has the following features:</p>
<p>Any suggestion is <a href="mailto:webmaster@bipm.org" target="_blank">welcome</a>.</p>
<ul><li>all definitions, notes, and examples of the VIM3 are taken without modification from the VIM3 itself;</li><li>the VIM3 Foreword, Introduction, Conventions, Scope, Annex, Bibliography, and List of acronyms are omitted;</li><li>an Alphabetical index is included;</li><li>in definitions, notes, and examples terms designating concepts defined in the VIM3 are hyperlinked;</li><li>some annotations are included, aimed at further clarification or comment on specific definitions, notes, or examples.</li></ul>
<p>  The JCGM decided that the annotations are informative and not normative.   They are exclusively developed by JCGM/WG2,   <a href="http://www.bipm.org/utils/en/pdf/JCGM_charter.pdf" target="_blank">according to procedures agreed by the JCGM</a>.</p>
