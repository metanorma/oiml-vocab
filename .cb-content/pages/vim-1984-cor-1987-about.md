<h2>VIM (1984/Cor.1:1987) — Corrigendum to the International Vocabulary of Metrology</h2>
<p>The <strong>1987 corrigendum</strong> to the <strong>International Vocabulary of Basic and General Terms in Metrology (VIM)</strong> was issued as <strong>OIML V 2:1984/Cor.1:1987</strong> (also published by ISO as <strong>ISO Guide 99:1984/Cor.1:1987</strong>). It corrects 3 of the 138 concepts in the <a href="/vocab/vim-1984/concept/1.01" target="_blank">1984 first edition</a>:</p>
<ul><li><strong>1.01 (measurable) quantity</strong> — Note 2 extended with "also called quantities of</li></ul>
<p>  the same kind" / « aussi appelées grandeurs de même nature ».</p>
<ul><li><strong>4.10 analogue measuring instrument</strong> — English definition "output or display"</li></ul>
<p>  changed to "output and/or display".</p>
<ul><li><strong>5.23 limits of (permissible) error</strong> — Preferred English designation extended</li></ul>
<p>  to "limits of (permissible) error" (adding the optional word "(permissible)").</p>
<p>The remaining 135 concepts are textually identical to V 2:1984 and linked to the original via <code>equivalent</code> edges. This dataset represents the <strong>canonical corrected form</strong> of V 2:1984 and is the reference target for <a href="/vocab/vim-1993/concept/1.1" target="_blank">OIML V 2:1993</a> supersession.</p>
