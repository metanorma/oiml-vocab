<h2>VIML (2013) — International Vocabulary of Legal Metrology</h2>
<p>The <strong>International Vocabulary of Legal Metrology (VIML)</strong> is published by the International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML) as <strong>OIML V 1:2013</strong>.</p>
<p>This is the <strong>2013 edition</strong>, superseded by <a href="/vocab/viml-2022/concept/0.01" target="_blank">OIML V 1:2022</a>. It provides 135 definitions of terms used in legal metrology across 8 sections.</p>
<h3>Structure</h3>
<table><thead><tr><th>Section</th><th>Title</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>0</td><td>Basic terms</td></tr><tr><td>1</td><td>Metrology and its legal aspects</td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td>Legal metrology activities</td></tr><tr><td>3</td><td>Documents and marks within legal metrology</td></tr><tr><td>4</td><td>Classification of measuring instruments</td></tr><tr><td>5</td><td>Construction and operation of measuring instruments</td></tr><tr><td>6</td><td>Software in legal metrology</td></tr><tr><td>A</td><td>Terms relating to conformity assessment</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Section A incorporates terms from <strong>ISO/IEC 17000:2004</strong> on conformity assessment.</p>
<h3>About OIML</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.oiml.org" target="_blank">International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML)</a> is an intergovernmental treaty organization that promotes the global harmonization of legal metrology procedures.</p>
