<h2>VIML (2000) — International Vocabulary of Legal Metrology</h2>
<p>The <strong>International Vocabulary of Legal Metrology (VIML)</strong> is published by the International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML) as <strong>OIML V 1:2000</strong>.</p>
<p>This is the <strong>2000 edition</strong>, superseded by <a href="/vocab/viml-2013/concept/0.01" target="_blank">OIML V 1:2013</a>. It provides 44 definitions of terms used in legal metrology across 4 sections.</p>

<h3>About OIML</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.oiml.org" target="_blank">International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML)</a> is an intergovernmental treaty organization that promotes the global harmonization of legal metrology procedures.</p>
