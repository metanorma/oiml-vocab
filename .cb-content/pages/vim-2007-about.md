<h2>VIM (2007) — International Vocabulary of Metrology</h2>
<p>The <strong>International Vocabulary of Metrology (VIM)</strong> is co-published by the Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM) as <strong>JCGM 200:2007</strong> and by OIML as <strong>OIML V 2-200:2007</strong>.</p>
<p>This is the <strong>2007 edition</strong>, superseded by <a href="/vocab/vim-2010/concept/1.1" target="_blank">OIML V 2-200:2010</a>. It provides 143 concept definitions across 5 chapters.</p>

<h3>About JCGM</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm" target="_blank">Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM)</a> maintains the VIM and the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM). The JCGM brings together BIPM, IEC, IFCC, ILAC, ISO, IUPAC, IUPAP, and OIML.</p>
