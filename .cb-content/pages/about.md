<h2>OIML Vocabularies of Metrology</h2>
<p>This site hosts interactive, browsable editions of the International Vocabularies of Metrology published by OIML and JCGM, plus the OIML Complete Vocabulary — a cross-publication terminology index derived from every OIML publication.</p>
<h3>VIML — International Vocabulary of Legal Metrology</h3>
<p>The <strong>International Vocabulary of Legal Metrology (VIML)</strong> is published by the International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML) as <strong>OIML V 1:2022</strong>.</p>
<p>This glossary provides the official definitions of terms used in legal metrology, covering measurement, measuring instruments, legal requirements, and conformity assessment.</p>
<p>The terminology is sourced from <strong>OIML V 1:2022</strong>, which incorporates definitions from:</p>
<ul><li><strong>OIML V 2-200:2012</strong> — International Vocabulary of Metrology (VIM)</li><li><strong>ISO/IEC 17000:2020</strong> — Conformity assessment vocabulary</li><li><strong>IEC 60050-300</strong> — International Electrotechnical Vocabulary</li></ul>
<h3>VIM — International Vocabulary of Metrology</h3>
<p>The <strong>International Vocabulary of Metrology (VIM)</strong> is published as <strong>JCGM 200:2012</strong> (3rd edition, also published as OIML V 2-200:2012). It defines basic and general concepts and associated terms used in metrology.</p>
<p>This online version is an abridged edition supplemented by informative annotations produced by Working Group 2 of the Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM/WG 2). It includes:</p>
<ul><li>all definitions, notes, and examples of the VIM3 without modification</li><li>hyperlinked cross-references between concepts</li><li>informative annotations by JCGM/WG 2 (marked with <code>[Annotation]</code>)</li></ul>
<p>The VIM3 PDF is available for free download from the <a href="https://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm" target="_blank">BIPM JCGM page</a>.</p>
<h3>About this site</h3>
<p>This online browser provides:</p>
<ul><li>interactive browsing and full-text search across all datasets</li><li>bilingual presentation (English and French)</li><li>hyperlinked cross-references between vocabularies</li><li>per-publication provenance for every term in the Complete Vocabulary</li><li>both alphabetical and analytical indexes</li></ul>
<h3>About OIML</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.oiml.org" target="_blank">International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML)</a> is an intergovernmental treaty organization that promotes the global harmonization of legal metrology procedures. Founded in 1955, OIML develops International Recommendations and Documents that provide model regulations for measuring instruments and measurement standards.</p>
