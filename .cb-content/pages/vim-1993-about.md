<h2>VIM (1993) — International Vocabulary of Metrology</h2>
<p>The <strong>International Vocabulary of Metrology (VIM)</strong> was originally published as <strong>ISO Guide 99:1993</strong> (also known as <strong>ISO/TAG 4:1993</strong>), co-published by OIML as <strong>OIML V 2:1993</strong> and by the JCGM predecessor as <strong>JCGM 200:1993</strong>.</p>
<p>This is the <strong>1993 edition</strong>, superseded by <a href="/vocab/vim-2007/concept/1.1" target="_blank">OIML V 2-200:2007</a>. It provides 120 concept definitions across 6 chapters. This edition uses a different chapter structure from later editions, with separate chapters for "Measurement results" and "Measuring instruments" instead of the consolidated "Devices for measurement" chapter.</p>

<h3>About JCGM</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm" target="_blank">Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM)</a> maintains the VIM and the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM). The JCGM brings together BIPM, IEC, IFCC, ILAC, ISO, IUPAC, IUPAP, and OIML.</p>
