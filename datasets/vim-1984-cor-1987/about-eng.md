# VIM (1984/Cor.1:1987) — Corrigendum to the International Vocabulary of Metrology

The **1987 corrigendum** to the **International Vocabulary of Basic and General Terms
in Metrology (VIM)** was issued as **OIML V 2:1984/Cor.1:1987** (also published by
ISO as **ISO Guide 99:1984/Cor.1:1987**). It corrects 3 of the 138 concepts in the
[1984 first edition](/vocab/vim-1984/concept/1.01):

- **1.01 (measurable) quantity** — Note 2 extended with "also called quantities of
  the same kind" / « aussi appelées grandeurs de même nature ».
- **4.10 analogue measuring instrument** — English definition "output or display"
  changed to "output and/or display".
- **5.23 limits of (permissible) error** — Preferred English designation extended
  to "limits of (permissible) error" (adding the optional word "(permissible)").

The remaining 135 concepts are textually identical to V 2:1984 and linked to the
original via `equivalent` edges. This dataset represents the **canonical corrected
form** of V 2:1984 and is the reference target for [OIML V 2:1993](/vocab/vim-1993/concept/1.1)
supersession.
