# VIM (1984) — International Vocabulary of Basic and General Terms in Metrology

The **International Vocabulary of Basic and General Terms in Metrology (VIM)** was
first published in **1984** as a joint publication by BIPM, IEC, ISO, and OIML,
issued by ISO as **ISO Guide 99:1984** (also known as **ISO/TAG 4:1984**) and by
OIML as **OIML V 2:1984**.

This is the **first edition**, superseded by [OIML V 2:1984/Cor.1:1987](/vocab/vim-1984-cor-1987/concept/1.01)
and ultimately by [OIML V 2:1993](/vocab/vim-1993/concept/1.1). It provides 138
concept definitions across 6 chapters. The 1984 edition uses a different chapter
structure from later editions, with separate chapters for "Measurement results"
and "Measuring instruments" instead of the consolidated "Devices for measurement"
chapter introduced in 2007.

**Note:** A corrigendum was issued in 1987 correcting 3 concepts (1.01, 4.10, 5.23).
This dataset preserves the original 1984 text; see [V 2:1984/Cor.1:1987](/vocab/vim-1984-cor-1987/concept/1.01)
for the corrected text.
